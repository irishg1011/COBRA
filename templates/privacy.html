3. Why we collect it

To create and secure your account, send verification and reset codes, show your lessons and track progress, recommend what to review, award badges and the certificate, let mentors and admins support learners, produce reports about how the course is used, and protect the system from misuse.

4. Our basis for using it

Your consent, given when you tick the box at sign-up or on the consent screen. For learners under 18, the consent of a parent or guardian, which the learner confirms by ticking the parent or guardian box. You may withdraw consent at any time; we will then close your account, because CobraByte cannot work without this data.

5. Cookies, login token and browser storage

When you sign in, we place a session cookie containing a random session token in your browser. It keeps you signed in and ends when you log out or after a period of inactivity. Your browser also stores a signed-in flag for the open tab and display preferences such as the theme. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Who can see your data

You. Mentors and admins of CobraByte, for teaching, support and security. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

7. Outside services

Verification emails are sent through Gmail, which receives your email address and the code. Lesson videos play from YouTube, which may set its own cookies and receives your IP address. Fonts, icons and the in-browser Python engine load from Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr, which receive your IP address. The site runs on a server from [hosting provider], located in [country].

8. How we protect it

Passwords are stored hashed. Access to learner data is limited to mentor and admin accounts. Accounts lock for a short time after repeated failed sign-ins. [The site uses HTTPS so data is encrypted in transit. Include this sentence only once it is true.]

Account and learning data: while your account is active, and deleted or made anonymous [2 years] after your last sign-in. Security records: [1 year]. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within [30 days], except records we must keep by law.

10. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you have the right to be informed, to object, to access your data, to correct it, to have it erased or blocked, to get a copy in a common electronic format, to claim damages, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To use any of these, write to [contact email]. We will reply within [15 days].

11. Children

CobraByte accepts learners from age 13. Learners under 18 confirm that a parent or guardian has read and agreed to the Terms and this notice. If a data breach affects a learner under 18, we will notify both the learner and the parent or guardian where we can reach them.

12. If something goes wrong

If a breach puts your data at real risk of serious harm, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours of learning about it.
